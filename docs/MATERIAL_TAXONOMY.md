# Material taxonomy

ThermoComposite Lab separates material chemistry, composite systems, reinforcement
architecture, scientific models, and saved calculations.

## Current catalog

The catalog recognises fibre-reinforced polymer, metal-matrix, ceramic-matrix,
carbon-carbon, hybrid, particulate, nanocomposite, and structural-composite
families. A catalog category is not a claim that a calculation is implemented.

Currently enabled calculations are limited to registered scalar-property systems:

- Continuous unidirectional fibre systems: effective properties, uniaxial thermal
  response, and supported plane-stress Classical Laminate Theory.
- Registered particulate systems: effective properties and uniaxial thermal response.

Woven, hybrid, sandwich, random/chopped, ceramic-matrix, carbon-carbon, and
nanocomposite workflows remain catalog-only until dedicated models and traceable
data are added.
